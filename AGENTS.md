# AI Production Instructions

## Mission

Create a professional, serialized, fictional manga with
consistent visual storytelling, characters, and continuity.

## Source of Truth

Read these files before creating story content:

1. series/index.md
2. series/bible.md
3. series/art-direction.md
4. characters/index.md
5. characters/_character-rules.md
6. Relevant character profiles
7. Relevant chapter continuity records

## Privacy

All public-facing material must be fictional.
Do not introduce identifying information about real individuals.
Do not import private journals, transcripts, conversations,
or unpublished source material into published content.

Use only approved fictional narrative details.

## Character Consistency

Maintain permanent physical traits and identifying accessories.
Use canonical references when generating artwork.
Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, or permanent changes.
Wardrobe and expressions may vary according to scenes.

## Story Consistency

Separate drafts from approved canon.
Track chronological events and relationship developments.
Do not silently rewrite established events.
Maintain character motivations across chapters.

## Production Pipeline

CONCEPT > OUTLINE > SCRIPT > STORYBOARD > ART > QA > APPROVED

Only approved material becomes canonical.

## Quality Gates

Check:
- Character appearance
- Character behavior
- Scene continuity
- Clothing continuity
- Lighting and environments
- Chronology
- Dialogue
- Panel reading order
- Lettering
- Image dimensions

## Approval

Significant canon changes require explicit approval.
Do not silently overwrite approved creative assets.

## Working Agreement

Update the relevant project files during each working session, including
character records, relationship details, continuity, and unresolved decisions.
Distinguish user-approved facts from new proposals awaiting approval.
When a story completes QA and receives approval, commit its approved files,
update continuity and the release index, and push to the configured GitHub
remote. Inspect the diff first and exclude private sources and unrelated work.
Do not treat a planning session or unfinished chapter as a completed story.
