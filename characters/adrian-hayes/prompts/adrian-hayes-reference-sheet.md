```text
Create an individual character reference sheet for the fictional manga series
Reflections. Use contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga with clean,
expressive linework, semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle
cel shading, readable facial detail, and realistic clothing folds. Adapt
anatomy to the character's actual age. Use soft, even studio lighting and a
plain neutral background so identity, proportions, and colors stay readable.

LAYOUT PROPOSAL
A 3:2 landscape study sheet: three full-body views, front, three-quarter, and
back, plus three head-and-shoulder expression studies. Give full-body views
the same scale and baseline. Keep feet and hair fully visible. Keep the
outfit, haircut, face, build, and accessory placement consistent in every
view. Each view depicts the same person; do not invent additional characters.
The proposed reference-sheet layout does not establish manga page standards.
Do not render labels, names, measurements, dialogue, watermarks, or logos.
CHARACTER
Adrian Hayes, a fictional adult, using the proposed age of 38 and height of
5'11" / approximately 180 cm for this design study. Thin, lanky build with
slightly rounded shoulders, realistic adult proportions, and an ordinary
neck length. Medium-length messy brown hair, hazel eyes, soft facial features,
and rectangular glasses. His occasional stubble is a draft grooming option;
use light stubble consistently across this particular sheet. Keep the same
provisional rectangular frame design in every view, with eyes readable through
the lenses. Proposed family resemblance draws on Morgan's eyes and nose;
preserve Adrian's own identity rather than copying Morgan. His complexion
is not specified in the written profile: use a supplied fictional reference,
or keep any proposed complexion explicitly provisional.

EXPRESSION AND PRESENCE
Socially hesitant, imaginative, intelligent, and sensitive, with enthusiasm
for gaming, Dungeons & Dragons, anime, miniature painting, and model hobbies.
Use a relaxed, slightly reserved stance without caricaturing awkwardness.
Expression studies: tentative neutral; an engaged smile suggesting interest
in a favorite imaginative subject; mild uncertainty with a thoughtful gaze.
His interests and friendships have value beyond family expectations.

Private gender questioning is a proposed internal direction; no final label,
pronoun change, disclosure, or family response is established. Possible autism
is a separate, undiagnosed and unestablished direction. Neither causes the

Do not depict either through invented physical markers, diagnostic poses,
clothing stereotypes, or exaggerated social awkwardness. His hobbies and
glasses do not establish a diagnosis or gender identity. Do not transfer

WARDROBE
Use one consistent draft outfit throughout: an oversized graphic hoodie,
cargo pants, and worn sneakers. Cargo pants select one existing draft option;
comfortable joggers remain an alternative for a separate study. Use a simple
abstract, nonverbal hoodie graphic without text, logos, or recognizable franchise
characters. Exact colors and graphic design remain provisional. Keep the fit
comfortable and natural. Omit hobby props and extra accessories so identity
stays readable. Rectangular glasses are a proposed design feature, not an
approved permanent accessory. No ring practice is established. Keep clothing,
graphics, glasses, and stubble identical across angles.

CONTINUITY
Age 38 and height 180 cm are study specifications, not finalized canon.
Keep his thin build, rounded shoulders, hair length, face, glasses, and eye
color consistent in every view. Do not exaggerate his lankiness into stretched
limbs or a childlike body. Adrian is the middle Hayes sibling, younger than
Nate and older than Morgan. Do not establish a diagnosis, final gender label,
new family incident, occupation, or disclosure through this sheet.
REFERENCE AND CONSISTENCY RULES
If fictional character references are attached, preserve their established
identity and reconcile them with the current written specifications above.
Do not copy outdated names, ages, heights, ring practices, or superseded
hairstyles from an older sheet. Flag a conflict with an approved reference
rather than silently changing either source. If no reference is supplied,
treat the result as a design study, not a claim to reproduce approved artwork.

Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, permanent markings, or signature
accessories. Avoid distorted hands, elongated necks, stretched limbs,
inconsistent eye colors, changing hair lengths, or different body types
between panels. Keep clothing fully appropriate to age and the scene.
Generate no other family members, environments, story events, or private
real-life details. New output requires review before it becomes a reference.
```
