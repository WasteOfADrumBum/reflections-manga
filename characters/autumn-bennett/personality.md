# Autumn Bennett: Personality

Status: Extroverted, outgoing, earthy, relaxed direction selected; detailed prior
suggestions remain proposals. Recorded 2026-11-13.

## User-Directed Core

Very outgoing and relaxed, providing a contrast to Ruby's reserved presentation.
The contrast does not remove Ruby's independence or give Autumn authority over her.

## Proposed Strengths and Motivation

Warm, physically affectionate, talkative, confident, funny, emotionally intuitive,
and comfortable meeting strangers. Wants connection and wants to encourage Ruby.

## Proposed Flaws and Mannerisms

- Assumes talking openly will resolve most difficulties.
- Can underestimate boundaries, overstay, or ask questions too directly.
- May offer encouragement when listening would help more.
- Mild flirtatiousness was suggested; it establishes no romantic intent or pairing.

## Proposed Sister Dynamic

Autumn adores Ruby but may slip into speaking for her younger sister. Ruby may
want affection without being managed. The earlier bold-older/quiet-younger
childhood contrast is a possible backstory, not an established family history.
No resentment, argument, visit, hug, or other chapter interaction has occurred
in canon merely because it appeared as an example in character development.
