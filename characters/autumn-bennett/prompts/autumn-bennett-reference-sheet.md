```text
Create an individual character reference sheet for the fictional manga series
Reflections. Use contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga with clean,
expressive linework, semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle
cel shading, readable facial detail, and realistic clothing folds. Adapt
anatomy to the character's actual age. Use soft, even studio lighting and a
plain neutral background so identity, proportions, and colors stay readable.

LAYOUT PROPOSAL
A 3:2 landscape study sheet: three full-body views, front, three-quarter, and
back, plus three head-and-shoulder expression studies. Give full-body views
the same scale and baseline. Keep feet and hair fully visible. Keep the
outfit, haircut, face, build, and accessory placement consistent in every
view. Each view depicts the same person; do not invent additional characters.
The proposed reference-sheet layout does not establish manga page standards.
Do not render labels, names, measurements, dialogue, watermarks, or logos.
CHARACTER
Autumn Bennett, a fictional adult woman and Ruby Bennett-Walker's older sister.
Her surname is confirmed as Bennett. Depict an adult approximately Morgan's age,
around 34, without finalizing her exact age. Same height as Ruby, approximately
152 cm / 5'0" using Ruby's current draft measurement. Fuller and slightly
heavier than Ruby, with a mature feminine figure and a fuller bust.
Maintain balanced, natural adult anatomy and an ordinary neck length.
Long strawberry-blonde hair is the suggested direction; use mid-back
length as a provisional choice. Strong family resemblance to Ruby.
Proposed shared features: heart-shaped face, softly rounded cheeks,
narrow chin, small slightly upturned nose, and expressive emerald-green
eyes, following Ruby's current visual draft. These details remain
provisional. Complexion and freckles are unselected.

EXPRESSION AND PRESENCE
Extroverted, outgoing, earthy, and relaxed. Draft extensions: warm,
talkative, confident, funny, and emotionally intuitive. Open, comfortable
full-body poses with easy warmth. Expression studies: relaxed attentive
neutral; a broad welcoming smile; gently concerned, engaged listening.
Keep her distinct from Ruby's quieter presence without exaggerating either.

WARDROBE
Use one consistent draft outfit throughout: wide-leg blue jeans, a loose
earthy-toned blouse, and plain ankle boots. Exact blouse color and cut are
provisional. Keep natural fabric movement and a comfortably fitted, fully
clothed silhouette, with identical garments and footwear across angles.
Do not automatically add the previously suggested rings, bracelets,
necklaces, stones, or crystals. Marital status and ring practice are
unestablished; Ruby's wedding-ring rule does not transfer to Autumn.

CONTINUITY
Preserve the sisters' same-height relationship, fuller versus slender
builds, and long strawberry-blonde versus copper-red pixie silhouettes.
Do not give Autumn Ruby's haircut, exact body, jewelry, or married surname.
If Ruby's fictional sheet is attached, use it only as a family-resemblance
guide, not an approved Autumn reference. Do not render Ruby or Avery.
The surname is Bennett, with two t's. No childhood event, occupation, or
romance is established.

REFERENCE AND CONSISTENCY RULES
If fictional character references are attached, preserve their established
identity and reconcile them with the current written specifications above.
Flag a conflict with an approved reference rather than silently changing
either source. If no reference is supplied, treat the result as a design
study, not a claim to reproduce approved artwork. All provisional choices
remain proposals; one study does not finalize undecided traits.

Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, permanent markings, or signature
accessories. Avoid distorted hands, elongated necks, stretched limbs,
inconsistent eye colors, changing hair lengths, or different body types
between views. Keep clothing fully appropriate to age and the scene.
Generate no other family members, environments, story events, or private
real-life details. New output requires review before it becomes a reference.
```
