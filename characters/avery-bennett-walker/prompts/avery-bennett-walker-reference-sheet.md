## Prompt

```text
Create an individual character reference sheet for the fictional manga series
Reflections. Use contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga with clean,
expressive linework, semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle
cel shading, readable facial detail, and realistic clothing folds. Adapt
anatomy to the character's actual age. Use soft, even studio lighting and a
plain neutral background so identity, proportions, and colors stay readable.

LAYOUT PROPOSAL
A 3:2 landscape study sheet: three full-body views, front, three-quarter, and
back, plus three head-and-shoulder expression studies. Give full-body views
the same scale and baseline. Keep feet and hair fully visible. Keep the
outfit, haircut, face, build, and accessory placement consistent in every
view. Each view depicts the same person; do not invent additional characters.
The proposed reference-sheet layout does not establish manga page standards.
Do not render labels, names, measurements, dialogue, watermarks, or logos.
CHARACTER
Avery Bennett-Walker, a fictional 7-year-old girl, 126 cm tall. Sturdy build
with natural, age-appropriate child proportions. Fair skin with a neutral
undertone. Noticeable freckles across the nose and cheeks. Hazel eyes with
brown, golden, and green tones. Long auburn hair, often tied back; for this
sheet choose a simple tied-back arrangement and keep it consistent. Mostly
resembles Ruby's heart-shaped face, rounded cheeks, and small nose, with a
distinct child facial identity. If Ruby's character reference is supplied,
use it for resemblance without copying adult proportions or her pixie cut.

EXPRESSION AND PRESENCE
Playful and expressive. Enjoys music, movement, sports, and climbing.
Expression studies: attentive neutral; a bright playful smile; mild
frustration while seeking help with a next step. Use natural body language
and hands without inventing a specific sign language, AAC device, or gesture
system. She is more physical than talkative and uses comfortable alternatives
before speech. This sheet needs no dialogue or speech bubbles. Do not portray
her as permanently silent, blank, distressed, or disengaged.

WARDROBE
Colorful graphic T-shirt and leggings in mixed-and-matched pastel colors.
Choose an original, simple, age-appropriate graphic without lettering,
brands, or logos. Exact graphics, color placement, footwear, and garment cuts
remain provisional design-study choices; no graphic becomes her permanent
signature automatically. Keep the same outfit across all views.

CONTINUITY
Age is confirmed at 7 and height at 126 cm, not age 11 or height 122 cm.
Long auburn hair must remain distinct from Ruby's copper-red pixie and
Riley's chestnut pixie. Autism is not a visible facial feature; do not invent
a physical marker, repetitive pose, medical equipment, or distress to signify
it. No particular sensory difficulty or support device is established.
For later family lineups she is only 4 cm shorter than Rowan and 16 cm taller
than Riley. Do not make her much smaller than Rowan because of birth order.
REFERENCE AND CONSISTENCY RULES
If fictional character references are attached, preserve their established
identity and reconcile them with the current written specifications above.
Do not copy outdated names, ages, heights, ring practices, or superseded
hairstyles from an older sheet. Flag a conflict with an approved reference
rather than silently changing either source. If no reference is supplied,
treat the result as a design study, not a claim to reproduce approved artwork.

Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, permanent markings, or signature
accessories. Avoid distorted hands, elongated necks, stretched limbs,
inconsistent eye colors, changing hair lengths, or different body types
between panels. Keep clothing fully appropriate to age and the scene.
Generate no other family members, environments, story events, or private
real-life details. New output requires review before it becomes a reference.
```
