# Caleb "Cal" Mercer: Personality

Status: Prior conversation proposals retained for development, not additional
approved history. Recorded 2026-11-13.

## Proposed Core Traits

Funny, blunt, extremely loyal, surprisingly perceptive, and comfortable with
dark humor. He can seem disengaged and then notice the thing Ethan is avoiding.

## Proposed Motivation and Strength

Offer Ethan companionship where uncertainty is safe to admit. Value autonomy
and being treated as a capable person rather than as fragile. These are
character directions, not an established recovery outcome or dependency on Ethan.

## Proposed Flaws and Mannerisms

- Procrastination, irregular sleep, and missed appointments.
- Withdrawing socially when struggling; resisting help that feels patronizing.
- Humor can deflect a serious conversation instead of helping it.

Smoking or heavy caffeine use were alternatives in the earlier proposal;
neither is selected. Do not introduce either habit automatically.

## Portrayal and Continuity

Recovery is part of the concept, not his entire personality. Do not equate
untidy clothing with substance use or infer current use, relapse, criminality,
violence, treatment, or a specific substance. No recovery milestone is established.
Exact voice, shared history, motivations, and future developments need review.
