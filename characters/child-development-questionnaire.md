## Inheritance Ground Rules

Hair color, eye color, and height involve multiple genes. Visible features do
not reveal a parent's complete genetic makeup or reliable inheritance odds.
There is no general rule assigning boys' looks to mothers or girls' looks to
fathers. Treat resemblance as a plausible artistic choice, not a genetic forecast.

Temperament involves genetic and environmental influences without a simple
maternal/paternal pattern. Habits, interests, expressions, and clothing can be
learned from any family member, including a non-biological parent. Haircuts
and wardrobe styles are choices. Keep child proportions appropriate to age.
