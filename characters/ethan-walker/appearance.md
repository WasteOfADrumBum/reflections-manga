# Ethan Walker: Appearance

Status: Draft v1

Adult man approximately 178 cm tall.

Moderately broad shoulders, naturally strong upper arms,
average chest, and a slightly rounded softer midsection.

Natural brown body hair on forearms and lower legs.

Brown high-fade undercut, closely cropped sides,
and a choppy, textured longer top.

Full, medium-long brown beard with a connected mustache.

Warm brown eyes, moderately square face,
and expressive eyebrows.

Normally carries himself with composed confidence.
Under emotional pressure, his shoulders become tense,
his gaze lowers, and his expression becomes guarded.

Maintain realistic adult proportions rather than
an exaggerated heroic physique.
