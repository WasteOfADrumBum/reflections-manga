```text
Create an individual character reference sheet for the fictional manga series
Reflections. Use contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga with clean,
expressive linework, semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle
cel shading, readable facial detail, and realistic clothing folds. Adapt
anatomy to the character's actual age. Use soft, even studio lighting and a
plain neutral background so identity, proportions, and colors stay readable.

LAYOUT PROPOSAL
A 3:2 landscape study sheet: three full-body views, front, three-quarter, and
back, plus three head-and-shoulder expression studies. Give full-body views
the same scale and baseline. Keep feet and hair fully visible. Keep the
outfit, haircut, face, build, and accessory placement consistent in every
view. Each view depicts the same person; do not invent additional characters.
The proposed reference-sheet layout does not establish manga page standards.
Do not render labels, names, measurements, dialogue, watermarks, or logos.
CHARACTER
Nathan "Nate" Hayes, a fictional adult man, using the proposed age of 41 and
height of 6'0" / approximately 183 cm for this design study. Stocky build,
broad shoulders, strong arms, and a slight belly. Use realistic adult
proportions and an ordinary neck length. Short dark-chestnut hair,
hazel-brown eyes, a heavy brow, square jaw, weathered appearance, and stubble.
Convey weathering through natural facial texture and expression, without
adding scars or permanent markings. Proposed family resemblance draws on
Diane's facial structure and Morgan's expressive eyes; preserve Nate's own
identity rather than copying either relative. His complexion is not specified
in the written profile: use a supplied fictional reference, or keep any

EXPRESSION AND PRESENCE
Protective, proud, hardworking, and emotionally guarded. His proposed
backstory is that he sincerely tried to lead and provide after their father,
who had alcoholism, left when Morgan was 13. He became overwhelmed, Morgan
took on responsibility, and he carries guilt about his perceived failure.
This detailed family response remains a proposal, not approved chronology.
Suggest a sturdy, outwardly assured stance with subtle tension rather than
an exaggerated display of masculinity. Expression studies: guarded neutral;
a small protective, affectionate smile; quiet self-doubt with a lowered gaze.
Preserve warmth and sincere effort alongside pride; do not make every pose angry.

WARDROBE

study so his build and haircut remain readable. No ring practice or permanent
accessory is established. Keep the outfit identical across angles.

CONTINUITY
Age 41 and height 183 cm are study specifications, not finalized canon.
Keep his stocky build, slight belly, short hair, stubble, face, and eye color
consistent in every view. Do not turn him into an extremely lean or exaggerated
muscular protagonist. Nate is the oldest Hayes sibling, followed by Adrian
and Morgan. Do not establish new family incidents, employment, or permanent
traits through this sheet.
REFERENCE AND CONSISTENCY RULES
If fictional character references are attached, preserve their established
identity and reconcile them with the current written specifications above.
Do not copy outdated names, ages, heights, ring practices, or superseded
hairstyles from an older sheet. Flag a conflict with an approved reference

treat the result as a design study, not a claim to reproduce approved artwork.

Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, permanent markings, or signature
accessories. Avoid distorted hands, elongated necks, stretched limbs,
inconsistent eye colors, changing hair lengths, or different body types
between panels. Keep clothing fully appropriate to age and the scene.
Generate no other family members, environments, story events, or private
real-life details. New output requires review before it becomes a reference.
```
