# Cassidy "Cass" Bennett: Personality

Status: Confirmed core direction, recorded 2026-11-19. Proposed elaborations remain separate.

## Confirmed Core

Confident, outspoken, friendly, humorous, and occasionally impatient. A mother,
teacher, and fitness enthusiast who is socially comfortable and genuinely likes Ruby.
Her directness can carry warmth and humor; impatience should not erase that warmth.

## Ruby's Aspirational Counterpart

Ruby admires Cass's confidence, independence, and social ease. Ruby's overwhelm
and hesitation impede follow-through, despite mutual interest in friendship.
Do not portray this as Cass rejecting Ruby or Ruby not caring. Cass retains
her own life rather than existing only to pursue or rescue Ruby.
Their current connection is friendly neighbors with potential for a closer friendship.

## Dialogue

Australian English is her natural speech style. Use appropriate vocabulary
and expressions selectively in context, without exaggerated accent spelling,
constant slang, or caricature. Her Australian nationality does not establish
a particular regional accent, hometown, or migration story.

## Development Proposals, Not Established Events

- Cass may occasionally speak before thinking, overcommit, or underestimate
  how difficult social follow-through feels to Ruby.
- Ruby might eventually see Cass's own exhaustion or uncertainty beneath her confidence.
- After friendly invitations, Cass might leave the door open and continue her own life.
- Running, strength training, kayaking, hiking, and girls' nights are possible
  interests beyond confirmed fitness, not a finalized hobby list or schedule.

No specific invitation, conversation, disappointment, revelation, or friendship
resolution is approved by these ideas.
