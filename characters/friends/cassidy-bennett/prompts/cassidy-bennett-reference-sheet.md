## Prompt

```text
Create ONE finished, full-color character reference-sheet study for the
fictional contemporary seinen manga series Reflections.

REQUIRED INPUT
Use the attached original fictional Cassidy Bennett character sheet as the
definitive reference for her face, body proportions, figure, skin tone,
freckles, brown eyes, long brunette hair, and existing tattoos. Preserve the
same identity, illustration style, and reference-sheet layout. If the sheet
is absent or tattoo placement is unreadable, request the required reference
instead of inventing a replacement design.

CHARACTER
Cassidy "Cass" Bennett, 40, Australian, a mother and elementary school teacher
who lives next door to the Walker family. Athletic and naturally curvy, with
realistic adult anatomy and the maturity of a fit 40-year-old woman. Exact
height is undecided; retain the reference proportions without height labels.
Her surname is Bennett, not Collins. A shared surname establishes no family
relationship with Ruby Bennett-Walker.

PRESENCE
Confident, outspoken, friendly, humorous, occasionally impatient. Use relaxed,
socially comfortable posture and readable, varied expressions while retaining
her facial construction. Her nationality does not require costume symbols.

OUTFIT
Reuse the foundational sheet's short-sleeve top and red shorts, including its
actual footwear and accessories. Keep outfit fit and all tattoo placements
consistent across views. Do not invent jewelry, a wedding ring, or permanent
markings. A later authorized outfit may change clothing and hairstyle only;
preserve hair length, face, proportions, and tattoos.

RENDERING AND QA
Contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga, clean expressive linework,
semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle cel shading, realistic
clothing folds, soft even lighting, and a neutral background. Keep full-body
views at the same scale and baseline; keep feet and hair visible. Preserve
tattoo motifs, anatomical side, scale, and placement; never mirror tattoos
or fabricate unreadable lettering. Do not copy incorrect old names or
unapproved household details from image labels. Do not add relatives or
story events. New output requires review before becoming an approved asset.
```
