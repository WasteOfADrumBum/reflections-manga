## Prompt

```text
Create an individual character reference sheet for the fictional manga series
Reflections. Use contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga with clean,
expressive linework, semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle
cel shading, readable facial detail, and realistic clothing folds. Adapt
anatomy to the character's actual age. Use soft, even studio lighting and a
plain neutral background so identity, proportions, and colors stay readable.

LAYOUT PROPOSAL
A 3:2 landscape study sheet: three full-body views, front, three-quarter, and
back, plus three head-and-shoulder expression studies. Give full-body views
the same scale and baseline. Keep feet and hair fully visible. Keep the
outfit, haircut, face, build, and accessory placement consistent in every
view. Each view depicts the same person; do not invent additional characters.
The proposed reference-sheet layout does not establish manga page standards.
Do not render labels, names, measurements, dialogue, watermarks, or logos.

CHARACTER
Ellie Greene, a fictional 9-year-old girl, Natalie's daughter and Owen's
older sister. Height is not established; use believable proportions for
a 9-year-old without assigning a measurement. Proposed design: a slender
child with long brown hair and an expressive face. Keep a natural child
head-to-body ratio, soft facial construction, ordinary neck length, and
age-appropriate hands and limbs. Provisional choices for this study:
straight brown hair worn loose below the shoulders, brown eyes, and no
glasses. Glasses remain an undecided option; omitting them here does not
establish a permanent rule. Complexion and detailed family resemblance
are unselected; use a supplied fictional reference or keep choices provisional.

EXPRESSION AND PRESENCE
Proposed personality: imaginative, cautious, bookish, and slightly dramatic.
Full-body poses should feel thoughtful and a little tentative, with quiet
curiosity rather than constant sadness. Expression studies: attentive
neutral; a bright smile as an idea occurs to her; mild uncertainty with
slightly drawn brows. Keep all three recognizably the same child.

WARDROBE
Use one consistent proposed outfit throughout: a muted teal cardigan over
a pale-yellow T-shirt, blue jeans, and plain sneakers. This is a draft
interpretation of colorful, slightly mismatched everyday clothing; the
garments and palette are not fixed canon. Keep colors, layers, fit, and
footwear identical across angles. No logos, readable graphics, jewelry,
school uniform, or extra props. A notebook was suggested as a possible
interest-related prop; omit it so the hands and silhouette remain visible.

CONTINUITY
Ellie is 9, the same age as Rowan, who is a boy. Shared age does not establish
the same height, facial features, school, or an existing friendship.
Do not copy Rowan's approved 130 cm measurement or design. Natalie and
Ellie's final appearances remain provisional; do not invent another parent
or treat suggested family resemblance as established genetics. No model
sheet or completed questionnaire is approved for Ellie.

REFERENCE AND CONSISTENCY RULES
If fictional character references are attached, preserve their established
identity and reconcile them with the current written specifications above.
Flag a conflict with an approved reference rather than silently changing
either source. If no reference is supplied, treat the result as a design
study, not a claim to reproduce approved artwork. Keep provisional choices
consistent within the sheet; they require review before becoming permanent.

Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, permanent markings, or signature
accessories. Avoid distorted hands, elongated necks, stretched limbs,
inconsistent eye colors, changing hair lengths, or different body types
between views. Use natural child anatomy and comfortable, age-appropriate
clothing throughout. Generate no other family members, environments, story
events, or private real-life details. New output requires review before
it becomes a reference.
```
