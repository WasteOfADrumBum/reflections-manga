## Prompt

```text
Create an individual character reference sheet for the fictional manga series
Reflections. Use contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga with clean,
expressive linework, semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle
cel shading, readable facial detail, and realistic clothing folds. Adapt
anatomy to the character's actual age. Use soft, even studio lighting and a
plain neutral background so identity, proportions, and colors stay readable.

LAYOUT PROPOSAL
A 3:2 landscape study sheet: three full-body views, front, three-quarter, and
back, plus three head-and-shoulder expression studies. Give full-body views
the same scale and baseline. Keep feet and hair fully visible. Keep the
outfit, haircut, face, build, and accessory placement consistent in every
view. Each view depicts the same person; do not invent additional characters.
The proposed reference-sheet layout does not establish manga page standards.
Do not render labels, names, measurements, dialogue, watermarks, or logos.

CHARACTER
Owen Greene, a fictional boy approximately 6 years old, Natalie's son and
Ellie's younger brother. His exact age and height remain undecided.
Use believable anatomy for an approximately-six-year-old without assigning
a height measurement. Proposed design: slender build and messy brown hair.
Keep a natural child head-to-body ratio, soft facial construction, ordinary
neck length, and age-appropriate hands and limbs. Provisional choices for
this study: short, tousled brown hair and brown eyes. Haircut details,
complexion, and detailed family resemblance are unselected; use a supplied
fictional reference or keep choices provisional. Keep the chosen hair
length, face, and eye color consistent across all views.

EXPRESSION AND PRESENCE
Proposed personality: energetic, curious, fearless, and interested in how
things work. Full-body poses should suggest alert curiosity through a
slight forward lean or ready-to-move stance while keeping the entire body
clearly readable. Expression studies: attentive curiosity; an excited,
open smile; puzzled concentration with lightly furrowed brows. Avoid
making every expression mischievous, aggressive, or exaggerated.

WARDROBE
Use one consistent proposed outfit throughout: a muted red T-shirt with
one simple abstract graphic containing no text, charcoal shorts, and plain
sneakers with tied laces. These garments and colors are draft choices,
not a fixed wardrobe. Keep the graphic shape and placement, clothing fit,
colors, footwear, and lace state identical across angles. No brands,
school uniform, jewelry, tools, machinery, or extra props. Earlier scraped
knees and untied laces were examples, not required character features;
do not add injuries or loose laces to this study.

CONTINUITY
Owen's age target matches Riley's approximately-6 age; do not finalize
either child's exact age through this sheet. Shared age does not establish
the same height, school, or an existing friendship. Do not copy Riley's
approved 110 cm measurement or design. Interest in taking things apart
does not establish an accident, advanced mechanical skill, or diagnosis.
No other parentage, approved model sheet, or completed questionnaire is
established for Owen.

REFERENCE AND CONSISTENCY RULES
If fictional character references are attached, preserve their established
identity and reconcile them with the current written specifications above.
Flag a conflict with an approved reference rather than silently changing
either source. If no reference is supplied, treat the result as a design
study, not a claim to reproduce approved artwork. Keep provisional choices
consistent within the sheet; they require review before becoming permanent.

Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, permanent markings, or signature
accessories. Avoid distorted hands, elongated necks, stretched limbs,
inconsistent eye colors, changing hair lengths, or different body types
between views. Use natural child anatomy and comfortable, age-appropriate
clothing throughout. Generate no other family members, environments, story
events, or private real-life details. New output requires review before
it becomes a reference.
```
