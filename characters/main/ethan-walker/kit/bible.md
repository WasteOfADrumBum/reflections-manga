## One-Line Summary

A quiet, analytical 38-year-old who carries responsibility for everyone's
happiness and has trouble saying what he needs. (I, P)

## Speech

- **Pace and rhythm:** thoughtful pauses before answering. (P)
- **Register:** short, plain sentences, often a few words: "Okay, squeeze in." "Bossy." "Start with the first one." (S)
- **Humor:** humor during uncomfortable situations (P); dry and playful with the kids, as in "Show me your meanest whiskers." (S)
- **Deflection:** steers toward practical tasks when feelings come up: "Hang on. I think the phone's crooked." "I thought we were trying to fix the picture." (S)
- **Inner voice vs spoken voice:** his thoughts are more honest than what he says out loud. Fits his tendency to conceal personal uncertainty. (P, S)
- **Pet names:** calls Morgan Hun; calls Ruby Babe or Baby. (I, R)

## Acting Notes

- Let him pause before he answers. Silence is part of his performance.
- Play the gap between what he thinks and what he says.
- He shows care by doing things, like fixing the shot, more than by saying things.
- Keep humor dry and low-key, never loud.
