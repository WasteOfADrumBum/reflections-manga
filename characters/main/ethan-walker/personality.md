# Ethan Walker: Personality

Status: Draft v1

The general traits below remain drafts. The relationship behavior in the
approved section is user-established fictional canon, recorded 2026-11-12.

Ethan is introverted, thoughtful, persistent, and analytical.

He is comfortable solving complex problems and can
adopt an outgoing, confident demeanor when needed.

## Strengths

- Technical problem solving
- Creativity
- Loyalty
- Observation
- Determination
- Taking responsibility

## Inner Conflicts

- Overthinking
- Self-doubt
- Difficulty relaxing
- Hesitation to express emotional needs
- Tendency to conceal personal uncertainty

## Mannerisms

- Thoughtful pauses
- Restless hands when anxious
- Occasional distant gaze
- Humor during uncomfortable situations
- Quiet reflection after social interaction

## Development

The user-directed series tension is responsibility for others' happiness
versus expressing needs and preserving identity. Allow hurt, confidence,
humor, mistakes, and accountability across both marriages. Neither his
submissive nor dominant role removes choice or responsibility. See the
[15-story plan](../../../series/roadmap.md); its incidents remain future drafts.

Future developments remain open; see [arc planning](../../../continuity/character-arcs.md).
