## Prompt

```text
Create an individual character reference sheet for the fictional manga series
Reflections. Use contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga with clean,
expressive linework, semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle
cel shading, readable facial detail, and realistic clothing folds. Adapt
anatomy to the character's actual age. Use soft, even studio lighting and a
plain neutral background so identity, proportions, and colors stay readable.

LAYOUT PROPOSAL
A 3:2 landscape study sheet: three full-body views, front, three-quarter, and
back, plus three head-and-shoulder expression studies. Give full-body views
the same scale and baseline. Keep feet and hair fully visible. Keep the
outfit, haircut, face, build, and accessory placement consistent in every
view. Each view depicts the same person; do not invent additional characters.
The proposed reference-sheet layout does not establish manga page standards.
Do not render labels, names, measurements, dialogue, watermarks, or logos.
CHARACTER
Morgan H. Walker, a fictional 34-year-old adult woman, approximately 170 cm
tall. Athletic, balanced proportions, moderately defined shoulders and arms,
a firm waist, and strong legs. Warm, lightly sun-kissed complexion with
subtle freckles. Softly angular face, defined cheekbones, natural feminine
jawline, straight nose, and almond-shaped hazel eyes. Layered chestnut-brown
hair extends two to three inches below the shoulders, with natural texture.
Maintain mature adult proportions.

EXPRESSION AND PRESENCE
Independent, practical, assertive, protective, and playful, with guarded
vulnerability. Confident posture and a direct gaze. Expression studies:
composed neutral; a dry amused smile with a raised eyebrow; thoughtful,
guarded concern. Show emotional range without making her automatically
hostile, cold, or the family's antagonist.

WARDROBE
Sporty tomboy and rugged country-alternative casualwear. Use one consistent
draft outfit throughout: a dark fitted T-shirt, relaxed dark jeans, a muted
olive utility jacket, and practical boots. These garment types and colors
come from her existing wardrobe draft. Keep the silhouette athletic and
practical. Morgan does not wear a wedding ring. Do not add a ring, invent
other jewelry, or turn wardrobe choices into permanent identifying traits.

CONTINUITY
Use Morgan H. Walker: Hayes is her middle name and Walker her married surname.
Do not hyphenate or treat Hayes Walker as a two-part surname. Use age 34, not 28.
Her haircut remains below the shoulders; Riley's pixie cut must not transfer
to Morgan. Her full relationship with Ethan and platonic friendship with Ruby
do not require any additional person to appear on this individual sheet.
No visual assumption about ring absence should imply betrayal or separation.
REFERENCE AND CONSISTENCY RULES
If fictional character references are attached, preserve their established
identity and reconcile them with the current written specifications above.
Do not copy outdated names, ages, heights, ring practices, or superseded
hairstyles from an older sheet. Flag a conflict with an approved reference
rather than silently changing either source. If no reference is supplied,
treat the result as a design study, not a claim to reproduce approved artwork.

Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, permanent markings, or signature
accessories. Avoid distorted hands, elongated necks, stretched limbs,
inconsistent eye colors, changing hair lengths, or different body types
between panels. Keep clothing fully appropriate to age and the scene.
Generate no other family members, environments, story events, or private
real-life details. New output requires review before it becomes a reference.
```
