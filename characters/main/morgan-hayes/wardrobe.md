# Morgan H. Walker: Wardrobe

Status: Draft v1

## Signature Style

Sporty tomboy, rugged country alternative,
with occasional understated feminine elements.

## Everyday

- Dark fitted shirts
- Oversized cropped tops
- High-waisted leggings
- Fitted or relaxed jeans
- High-top canvas sneakers
- Practical boots

## Outerwear

- Denim jackets
- Utility jackets
- Dark leather jackets
- Flannel overshirts

## Occasional Outfits

- Flowing dark skirts
- Lace-trimmed tops
- Western boots

## Palette

Black, charcoal, olive, navy, burgundy,
brown, and muted cream.

## Rules

Morgan does not wear a wedding ring. Do not add one to new artwork.
Her reason for not wearing it has not been established.

Practical comfort takes priority.
Retain an athletic, recognizably tomboy silhouette.
