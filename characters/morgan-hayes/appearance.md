# Morgan Hayes: Appearance

Status: Draft v1

Adult woman, approximately 170 cm tall.

Athletic and balanced proportions, moderately defined
shoulders and arms, firm waist, and strong legs.

Warm, lightly sun-kissed complexion with subtle freckles.

Softly angular face, defined cheekbones, natural feminine
jawline, straight nose, and almond-shaped hazel eyes.

Chestnut-brown hair extends approximately two to three
inches below her shoulders, with natural layered texture.

Confident posture and direct gaze.

Expressions range from amused smirks and raised brows
to thoughtful concern and guarded vulnerability.
