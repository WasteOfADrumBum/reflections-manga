# Morgan Hayes: Personality

Status: Draft v1

Morgan is independent, practical, assertive, and playful.

She approaches challenges directly and values autonomy.
Her confident exterior sometimes conceals uncertainty.

## Mannerisms

- Direct eye contact
- Raised eyebrows
- Dry, teasing humor
- Relaxed stance
- Arms crossed when guarded
- Thoughtful silence under pressure

## Strengths

- Resourceful
- Protective
- Determined
- Creative
- Dependable

## Flaws

- Can become defensive
- Hesitates to ask for help
- May withdraw when overwhelmed
- Sometimes hides uncertainty behind humor
