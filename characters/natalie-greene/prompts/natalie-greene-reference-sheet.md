## Prompt

```text
Create an individual character reference sheet for the fictional manga series
Reflections. Use contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga with clean,
expressive linework, semi-realistic construction, natural anatomy, subtle
cel shading, readable facial detail, and realistic clothing folds. Adapt
anatomy to the character's actual age. Use soft, even studio lighting and a
plain neutral background so identity, proportions, and colors stay readable.

LAYOUT PROPOSAL
A 3:2 landscape study sheet: three full-body views, front, three-quarter, and
back, plus three head-and-shoulder expression studies. Give full-body views
the same scale and baseline. Keep feet and hair fully visible. Keep the
outfit, haircut, face, build, and accessory placement consistent in every
view. Each view depicts the same person; do not invent additional characters.
The proposed reference-sheet layout does not establish manga page standards.
Do not render labels, names, measurements, dialogue, watermarks, or logos.
CHARACTER
Natalie "Nat" Greene, a fictional 34-year-old adult woman, the same age as
Morgan, approximately 178 cm tall, about 5'10". Tall, thin, and slightly
lanky, with balanced human proportions and an ordinary neck length. Dorky
with glasses; less tomboyish than Morgan, practical and casually feminine.
Provisional choices for this study: medium-brown straight shoulder-length
hair, brown eyes, and slightly oversized rounded-rectangular glasses.
Keep hair, eyes, and frame shape consistent in every view. These choices
do not finalize the earlier hair, eye, or frame alternatives. Complexion
and facial details are unselected; use a supplied fictional reference or
keep any new choices provisional.

EXPRESSION AND PRESENCE
Draft personality: friendly, nerdy, observant, sarcastic, slightly anxious,
and easy to talk to one-on-one. Relaxed full-body poses with mild social
awkwardness and a welcoming gaze. Expression studies: attentive neutral;
a small amused smile; thoughtful concern with slightly raised brows.
Keep facial expressions readable behind her glasses.

WARDROBE
Use one consistent draft outfit throughout: a simple muted knit sweater,
straight-leg blue jeans, plain sneakers, and her glasses. Exact sweater
color and frame design are provisional. Keep clothing fit, frames, and
footwear identical across angles. Avoid readable shirt graphics, labels,
or brand marks. No signature jewelry, bag, or extra props. Romantic status
and ring practice are undecided; do not establish them through accessories.

CONTINUITY
She is Morgan's best friend outside the household; Morgan and Ruby retain
their existing friendship. Nat's children are Ellie, 9, and Owen,
approximately 6; do not include them in this individual sheet. Her height
matches Ethan's current draft and exceeds Morgan's current 170 cm draft.
Do not stretch her limbs, make her underweight, or substitute Morgan's
athletic build. Her final hair, eyes, complexion, and glasses design require
review before becoming permanent character traits.

REFERENCE AND CONSISTENCY RULES
If fictional character references are attached, preserve their established
identity and reconcile them with the current written specifications above.
Flag a conflict with an approved reference rather than silently changing
either source. If no reference is supplied, treat the result as a design
study, not a claim to reproduce approved artwork. All provisional choices
remain proposals; one study does not finalize undecided traits.

Do not invent tattoos, scars, piercings, permanent markings, or signature
accessories. Avoid distorted hands, elongated necks, stretched limbs,
inconsistent eye colors, changing hair lengths, or different body types
between views. Keep clothing fully appropriate to age and the scene.
Generate no other family members, environments, story events, or private
real-life details. New output requires review before it becomes a reference.
```
