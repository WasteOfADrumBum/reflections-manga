# Ruby Bennett: Personality

Status: Draft v1

Ruby is gentle, affectionate, perceptive, and quietly romantic.

She communicates through sincere attention, soft gestures,
and subtle facial expressions.

Common mannerisms:
- Warm, genuine smiles
- Slight head tilts when listening
- Hands clasped when nervous
- Graceful hand movements
- Bashful expressions when embarrassed

She values connection and emotional openness.
Her quiet demeanor does not prevent independent decisions.

## Character Flaws

- Hesitates during confrontation
- Can overthink emotional conversations
- Sometimes avoids expressing disappointment
