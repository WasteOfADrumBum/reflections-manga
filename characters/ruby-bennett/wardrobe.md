# Ruby Bennett: Wardrobe

Status: Draft v1

## Signature Style

Feminine, ballet-inspired, soft romantic casualwear.

## Everyday

- Fitted knit tops
- Soft cardigans
- High-waisted pencil skirts
- Comfortable yoga leggings
- Ballet flats and ankle boots

## Formal Casual

- Tailored skirts
- Elegant blouses
- Fitted sweaters
- Modest jewelry

## Palette

Ivory, blush, dusty rose, muted lavender,
burgundy, warm cream, and charcoal.

## Rules

Preserve feminine silhouettes and natural fabric movement.
Outfits may vary by scene, weather, and activity.
