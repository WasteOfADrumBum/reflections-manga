## User-Directed Series Journeys

The following are intended journeys, not claims that their proposed events or
resolutions have already happened:

| Character | Planned emotional throughline | Preserve throughout |
|---|---|---|
| Ethan | Express needs and retain identity while experiencing different roles with each spouse; take responsibility for his own part in conflict | Agency in both relationships, parenting bonds, humor, and interests |
| Morgan | Explore independence/control alongside tenderness, guilt, support, reassurance, and trust | Capacity for affection, consent, interiority, and a life beyond conflict |

| All three children | Participate meaningfully in the ongoing household without carrying the adults' emotional repair | Established interests, communication, ages/heights, sibling bonds, and individual needs |
| Morgan and Ruby | Develop their own friendship through private talks, disagreement, tenderness, and parenting decisions | Platonic, nonsexual bond independent of Ethan's presence |

The six S-tier concepts anchor the trajectory. Supporting concepts develop
affection, trust, privacy, identity, and everyday repair between larger turns.
