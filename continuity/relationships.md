## Relationship History

- All three worked in the same building in different jobs and knew one another
  there. They began spending time together outside work.
- Ethan was attracted to both women. Ruby wanted a romantic, emotionally close
  relationship with Ethan; Morgan's initial attraction was more physical.
- Ethan and Morgan began a physical relationship first. Ethan and Ruby grew
  emotionally closer during the same general period.
- As intimacy with Ruby grew, the three discussed and established a consensual
  polyamorous relationship. Polyamory was popular in the area where they lived;
  the area itself remains unnamed.
- Approximately six months after establishing that relationship, they moved
  in together. This is a relative interval, not a calendar date.
- Their relationships continued together into their shared marital commitment.
  The interval from moving in to the ceremony remains unspecified.
