# Intimacy and Mature Content Guidelines

**Project:** Reflections  
**Status:** Production guidance  
**Scope:** Adult characters, intimate scenes, visual storytelling, and manga illustration

## Purpose

Reflections is a mature, character-driven seinen manga exploring love, marriage, emotional vulnerability, affection, power dynamics, family relationships, and personal identity.

Physical intimacy is a natural part of its storytelling. Certain scenes may feature boudoir-inspired imagery, revealing clothing, romantic physical contact, or implied sexual intimacy.

These scenes should support character development, emotional authenticity, and narrative progression rather than existing solely for sexual stimulation.

## Approved Visual Approaches

Adult intimate scenes may incorporate:

- Lingerie and tasteful boudoir clothing
- Robes, nightgowns, sleepwear, and loose-fitting garments
- Partially unbuttoned shirts and loosened clothing
- Romantic kissing, embraces, cuddling, and affectionate touching
- Characters lying or sitting together in bed
- Implied undressing without explicit anatomical exposure
- Bodies modestly covered by blankets, sheets, or clothing
- Silhouettes and carefully controlled lighting
- Reflections in mirrors and intimate close-up compositions
- Clothing placed beside furniture or on the floor
- Aftermath and post-intimacy moments
- Facial expressions conveying desire, trust, hesitation, tenderness, or vulnerability

## Visual Boundaries

The intended visual style emphasizes mature romance and non-explicit intimacy.

- Avoid explicit depictions of sexual acts.
- Avoid graphic anatomical exposure.
- Use composition, lighting, clothing, expressions, and body language to suggest intimacy.
- Establish consent and emotional context clearly.
- Keep every character's established appearance and proportions consistent.
- Apply romantic and boudoir depiction guidelines exclusively to clearly established adult characters.
- Never sexualize minors or depict minors participating in sexual activity.

## Narrative Priorities

Each intimate scene should communicate at least one meaningful story element:

1. **Emotional connection:** Trust, affection, reassurance, or vulnerability.
2. **Relationship dynamics:** Leadership, surrender, confidence, or mutual support.
3. **Character development:** Personal needs, insecurity, self-discovery, or changing expectations.
4. **Conflict and resolution:** Rejection, reconciliation, longing, or emotional distance.
5. **Realistic intimacy:** The ordinary physical closeness that accompanies adult relationships.

## Manga Art Direction

Maintain the established contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired presentation.

Use expressive linework, semi-realistic anatomy, natural poses, subtle shading, believable clothing folds, cinematic framing, and consistent character references.

Intimate sequences should prioritize visual storytelling through panel composition, facial detail, body language, and environmental context.

## Production Workflow

1. Develop the scene's narrative purpose and emotional progression.
2. Confirm the participating characters are adults.
3. Prepare individual panel descriptions using approved visual approaches.
4. Reference canonical character sheets for appearance and continuity.
5. Generate or illustrate artwork using a production method compatible with the scene's requirements.
6. Review visual consistency, emotional intent, consent, and content boundaries.
7. Assemble approved artwork into the manga chapter.

**Rendering platform:** Undecided. No external image-generation provider is mandated.

## Canon and Continuity

This document establishes production guidance only. It does not authorize new story events, character relationships, clothing preferences, or intimate encounters.

All actual scenes remain subject to the approved story outlines, character biographies, continuity records, and chapter production process.
