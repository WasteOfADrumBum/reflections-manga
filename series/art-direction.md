# Visual Style Guide

Status: Draft v1

## Primary Style

Contemporary Japanese seinen-inspired manga.

## Visual Characteristics

- Semi-realistic adult proportions
- Clean expressive linework
- Detailed but readable faces
- Natural body language
- Subtle cel shading
- Realistic clothing folds
- Controlled cinematic lighting
- Emotionally expressive composition

## Character Consistency

Refer to the canonical character profiles and approved
model sheets for facial structure, hairstyles, proportions,
accessories, and clothing.

Do not randomize canonical physical traits.

### Child Specifications

Use the approved written profiles until model sheets are created and approved:

- [Rowan](../characters/main/rowan-hayes-walker/appearance.md): 9, 130 cm, slender;
  chestnut textured short hair, brown-leaning hazel eyes, no visible freckles.
- [Riley](../characters/main/riley-hayes-walker/appearance.md): Approximately 6, 110 cm, slender;
  chestnut pixie cut, green-leaning hazel eyes, light nose freckles.
- [Avery](../characters/main/avery-bennett-walker/appearance.md): 7, 126 cm, sturdy;
  long auburn hair often tied back, hazel eyes, noticeable nose/cheek freckles.

Use child anatomy and age-appropriate proportions. Rowan is only 4 cm taller
than Avery; do not exaggerate that difference. Riley is distinctly shorter.
User-supplied child sheets are now registered in each character's references folder; use them with the approved written specifications and reference notes.
Do not depict autism through invented facial or physical markers.

## Composition

- Maintain consistent panel reading order
- Establish environments before close-ups
- Use close-ups for subtle emotional changes
- Reserve negative space for dialogue
- Keep backgrounds and lighting continuous within scenes

## Intimacy and Mature Content

Apply the shared [Intimacy and Mature Content Guidelines](../production/intimacy-and-mature-content.md)
across all 15 interconnected stories and their chapters, from scene planning
through artwork and QA. Maintain that document as the single source for this
production guidance instead of repeating it in individual chapters.
It does not approve story events or replace character canon, continuity,
or the chapter approval process.

## Color

Muted, natural color palette with restrained highlights.
Allow chapter-specific mood lighting.

Retain the children's approved wardrobe colors under the scene's lighting:
Rowan's blue/orange, Riley's purple/teal, and Avery's mixed pastels.
Restrained rendering does not replace these palettes with adult wardrobe colors.
