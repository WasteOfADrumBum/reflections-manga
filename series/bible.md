# Series Bible

Status: Draft v1

## Premise

Reflections follows three fictional adults navigating
different approaches to affection, independence, and identity.

Through ordinary interactions and emotionally significant
moments, the narrative explores how perception, expectation,
and communication shape relationships.

## Narrative Style

- Emotionally grounded
- Character driven
- Mature and introspective
- Subtle psychological storytelling
- Believable interpersonal conflict
- Occasional humor and warmth

## Perspective

Scenes may alternate between external observations,
internal monologues, memories, and imagined possibilities.

Clearly distinguish objective events from subjective thoughts.
