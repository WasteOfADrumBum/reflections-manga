# Series Bible

Status: Draft v1

## Premise

Reflections is planned as 15 interconnected stories within one ongoing family
continuity, grouped into four arcs. The six S-tier concepts carry the main
emotional journey; the other nine deepen it. See the [connected roadmap](roadmap.md)
for stable story IDs, reading order, and draft plot outcomes.

Central series question: How can someone be the person everyone needs, without
losing the person they actually are?

Reflections follows three fictional adults navigating
different approaches to affection, independence, and identity.

Ethan Walker (38), Morgan H. Walker (34), and Ruby Bennett-Walker (28)
share a committed polyamorous marriage. Ethan has a full romantic relationship
with each woman. Morgan and Ruby are close platonic friends, with no romantic
or sexual relationship to one another. Their friendship includes private
conversations, mutual care, and imperfect communication.

They met through different jobs in the same building and began socializing
outside work. Ethan and Morgan's physical relationship began first while Ethan
and Ruby grew emotionally closer. The three established consensual V-polyamory
and moved in together approximately six months later. All three married in a
shared same-day ceremony, with Ethan standing between Morgan and Ruby.
Their binding personal commitment is established; legal recognition is unspecified.

Morgan is more dominant with Ethan, whose approach with her is more submissive,
direct, and joking. Ethan is more dominant with Ruby, whose approach is more
submissive; they share deep, meaningful conversations. Morgan and Ruby are best
friends and coparents. Ethan works full-time, Morgan works part-time and is the
firmer disciplinary mother, and Ruby handles most daily care for all three children.

Ethan and Morgan have a son, about 9, and a daughter, about 6.

## Narrative Style

### User-Directed Character Framework

Ethan can feel submissive and overwhelmed with Morgan while being confident,
protective, and dominant with Ruby. His pressure to satisfy everyone and
difficulty expressing needs form the central identity tension. He still
contributes to conflicts and must retain agency in both relationships.

Morgan's strong will, passion, directness, and desire for independence coexist
with a need for tenderness, support, reassurance, and understanding. Ruby's
nurturing affection and desire for harmony coexist with needs, boundaries,
and possible resentment of always mediating. Neither woman's role is fixed
to conflict or comfort. These guide characterization; specific incidents and
changes remain draft story material until approved.

Dominance/submission remains consensual and does not decide whose voice
matters or who carries the family. Morgan and Ruby have meaningful private
conversations, disagreements, tenderness, and shared parenting as platonic
best friends. Ruby is particularly attuned to Avery's support needs; this
does not change Avery's established closest attachment to Ethan or exclude
the other adults from parenting.

### Storytelling Treatment

- Emotionally grounded
- Character driven
- Mature and introspective
- Subtle psychological storytelling
- Believable interpersonal conflict
- Occasional humor and warmth
