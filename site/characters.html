Ethan Walker

The one everyone leans on

  • Age 38
  • 5'10" / 178 cm
  • Husband to Morgan and Ruby
  • Works full-time

Ethan is technically minded and quietly introspective, a dependable problem solver who keeps his own uncertainty to himself. He's analytical, protective and observant, and he'd rather crack a joke than admit he's struggling.

With Morgan, he tends to follow her lead; their relationship is direct, funny and quick to spark into arguments. With Ruby, he's the one who leads, and they share deep, unhurried conversations. He calls Morgan “Hun” and Ruby “Babe.”

His central struggle: feeling responsible for everyone's happiness while finding it hard to say what he needs, and wondering who he is when no one needs anything from him.

At a glance

  • Analytical and observant
  • Loyal and determined
  • Overthinks and doubts himself
  • Thoughtful pauses; restless hands when anxious

Morgan Hayes-Walker

Strong-willed, and more tender than she lets on

  • Age 34
  • 5'7" / 170 cm
  • Married to Ethan
  • Works part-time

Morgan is confident, practical and fiercely independent. She meets problems head on, teases with a dry sense of humor, and hides uncertainty behind it more often than she'd admit.

She takes the lead with Ethan, and their chemistry comes with plenty of clashes. Ruby is her best friend; the two confide in each other and co-parent all three kids. She's the firmer parent at home, and Riley is closest to her.

Her inner tension: wanting independence and control while also needing support, tenderness and understanding. She doesn't wear her wedding ring, and she hasn't said why.

At a glance

  • Assertive and resourceful
  • Protective and playful
  • Can get defensive
  • Hesitates to ask for help

Ruby Bennett-Walker

The quiet heart of the house

  • Age 28
  • 5'0" / 152 cm
  • Married to Ethan
  • Handles most daily childcare

Ruby is warm, gentle and quietly romantic. She speaks through attention, small gestures and the expressions that cross her face, and she moves with a former dancer's grace.

She looks to Ethan for reassurance and direction, and their bond is built on emotional closeness and long talks. Morgan is her best friend and co-parent. Ruby stepped back to focus on the kids and handles most of their daily care; Rowan is closest to her, and she's especially attuned to what Avery needs.

Her inner tension: she wants harmony and closeness, but she struggles to voice her own needs and can come to resent always being the one who keeps the peace.

At a glance

  • Affectionate and perceptive
  • Emotionally attentive
  • Hesitates during confrontation
  • Avoids showing disappointment

Rowan Hayes-Walker

The builder with a comeback for everything

  • Age 9
  • 130 cm
  • Son of Ethan and Morgan
  • Closest to Ruby

Rowan is sociable, talkative and funny, and he loves making people laugh. He's happiest building things: puzzles, construction toys, and figuring out how mechanisms work.

When something feels unfair he argues his case, and his excitement sometimes outruns his judgment. He's picked up Morgan's raised eyebrows and dry humor, and he and his little sister Riley are nearly inseparable.

At a glance

  • Sociable and funny
  • Loves building and puzzles
  • Acts before thinking

Riley Hayes-Walker

Fearless, curious, and doing it herself

  • Age about 6
  • 110 cm
  • Daughter of Ethan and Morgan
  • Closest to Morgan

Riley is fearless and inquisitive, always eager to investigate and try things on her own. She loves pretend play, costumes and cats.

When she's frustrated she insists on doing it alone, and accepting help is hard for her. She has Morgan's playful confidence, and she and her big brother Rowan are almost always together.

At a glance

  • Fearless and inquisitive
  • Loves pretend play, costumes and cats
  • Finds it hard to accept help

Avery Bennett-Walker

All motion, music and big feelings

  • Age 7
  • 126 cm
  • Daughter of Ethan and Ruby
  • Closest to Ethan

Avery is playful and expressive. She loves jokes, music, movement, sports and climbing, and she shares her excitement and feelings freely.

Avery is autistic. She's more physical than talkative and often prefers other ways of communicating before speech. Advance notice and a visual plan help with transitions, and when things get overwhelming, a trusted adult nearby helps. When she's frustrated she wants help finding the next step, and compromise can be tough when a plan isn't what she wanted.

She's learned Ethan's care in explaining things. Rowan and Riley's tight pairing sometimes leaves her on the outside, but all three still love playing together.

At a glance

  • Playful and expressive
  • Loves music, sports and climbing
  • Finds compromise hard
