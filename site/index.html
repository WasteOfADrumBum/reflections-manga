Reflections

A character-driven drama about one family: three adults in a committed marriage, three kids, and the quiet work of being truly known by the people you love.

How can someone be the person everyone needs, without losing the person they actually are?

Explore the series Meet the characters

The story

Ethan, Morgan and Ruby share one home and one marriage. Ethan is married to both women. Morgan and Ruby are best friends and co-parents, raising Rowan, Riley and Avery together, with all three kids seeing all three adults as their parents.

Ethan loves two very different women, and he becomes a different version of himself with each. Morgan expects him to trust her lead. Ruby looks to him for reassurance and direction. Both love him, and he starts to wonder whether either of them knows the man he is when he's alone.

Through ordinary days and turning points, Reflections looks at how perception, expectation and communication shape a family, told across 15 connected stories in four arcs.

What it's about

Mature, introspective storytelling that stays grounded in believable people and believable conflict, with room for humor and warmth.

  • Identity and self-perception
  • Communication and misunderstanding
  • Emotional connection
  • Independence and vulnerability
  • Memory and interpretation
  • Growth and acceptance

Inside and out

Scenes move between what everyone can see, inner monologue, memory and imagined possibilities, always clear about which is which.

One continuous family

Every story carries forward. Trust, fatigue, boundaries and repair build over time instead of resetting at the end of an episode.

Everyone has a voice

Ethan needs agency, Morgan needs tenderness, Ruby needs boundaries. No one exists only to cause conflict or to soothe it.

Four arcs

The planned road through the series. Story outlines are in development and may change.

Arc I

Three Become One

The marriage, the personalities, the affection, and the needs no one has said out loud.

Arc II

The Invisible Weight

Parenthood, exhaustion, communication and loneliness.

Arc III

Fractured Reflections

Past damage, intimacy, boundaries and personal identity.

Arc IV

The Choice

The future of the family, and whether all three choose to continue together.

Reflections. All characters and events are fictional.
