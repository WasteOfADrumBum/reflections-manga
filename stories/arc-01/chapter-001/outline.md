## Logline

Ethan moves between the different roles he enjoys with Morgan and Ruby,
then begins to recognize the needs he hides and takes a small step toward
expressing them within their loving family.

## Emotional Arc

Warmth and belonging → private uncertainty → competing needs → recognition
of authentic parts of himself → tentative boundaries and continued inquiry.
