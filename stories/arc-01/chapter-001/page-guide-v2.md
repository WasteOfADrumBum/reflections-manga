## Speech and private thoughts

In selected conversations from page 6 onward, let the reader hear a character's spoken answer and then see the thought they keep to themselves. Use the existing oval balloon for the spoken line and a separate cloud with dots to the thinker for the private line. Follow the prompt's reading order. The other characters can notice a pause, a tight grip, or a hand drawn back. Their responses should follow what they heard and saw. Let quieter exchanges and reactions carry the scene between those moments.

On page 13, Ethan covers his anger about Morgan answering for him. Ruby hears the brush-off and holds back from comforting him. Pages 15 and 28 carry that tension through Morgan's growing awareness of the same habit.

## Continuity for the regeneration

- Pages 1–5 establish the shared wedding, biological parentage, both mothers parenting across those lines, the women's friendship and the three adults' private worries. Ruby keeps her band on. Morgan's left ring finger is bare. The later marriage conversation remains open.
- Page 5 ends with Ethan starting the timer and jogging toward his spot. He cancels that attempt on page 6. They adjust the phone on page 9 and take the first group photo on page 10. Avery chooses a place, hears the two-step plan and teaches the others her rhythm. Ethan describes periods of low mood in his own words.
- Pages 11–18 follow two distinct photos and the disagreement they provoke. Keep the selected panels from 10 and 12 as photo references. All six appear in the first. Morgan and the three children appear in the second. They return indoors together for water.
- Pages 19–27 move through afternoon, after bedtime and out onto the porch that evening. Give the friends a private conversation and Ethan a different moment alone with each spouse.
- Pages 28–30 return to the closed album the next morning. The empty pocket and Morgan's question carry into the next chapter. The last thought is Ethan's: “I know how to be needed…”

English, LEFT TO RIGHT across each row, then down. Place the first text unit highest at the left, the reply lower to its right. Frame speakers in that order before drawing balloons. SPEECH uses a smooth oval and one pointed tail aimed at the visible speaker's mouth. THOUGHT uses a scalloped cloud and a short chain of diminishing dots ending beside its owner's head. NARRATION alone uses a square-cornered, tailless box. Print only the quoted lines, exactly once and in listed order. Names, types and panel numbers are instructions. Render only assigned lettering. Keep tails and dot trails clear of other faces. Leave generous white space for lettering. No printed speaker labels, page title or added SFX.
