## Draft Continuity Choices

The new card, toy bridge, lavender sneakers, two gray folding chairs and exact
interactions are scene proposals. Avery's card is a simple transition plan
matching existing support preferences, not a selected AAC system; play and short
chosen speech do not define a diagnosis, sensory trigger or support level.
Children do not repair the adults' conflict. Rowan's coffee line leaves the
still-open child-to-Ethan address out of the dialogue.

The 16–20 sequence creates ordinary competing requests. Morgan carries her own
chair and leaves room to change the order; Ruby's wish to talk still matters.
Ethan's interpretation of "It's okay" is his thought, not objective mind-reading.
