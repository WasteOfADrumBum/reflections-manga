# Story 001: Opening Page Prompt Pack

Status: DRAFT v1 for manual generation and review, 2026-11-13.
Source: [Opening script](script.md), pages 001–004, 18 panels.
Purpose: Four self-contained prompts for the user to paste with character images.
No artwork generated, no script or scene approved, and no model sheet replaced.

## How to Use

Generate one page at a time, ideally in the same conversation. Upload the actual
character images in the order listed inside each prompt. A name or local path
does not attach an image. Use the same identity sheet version whenever a
character returns; the registered everyday v1 cards below are the starting set.
Do not switch to wedding, gym, swimwear, or sleepwear sheets between pages.

After accepting a page as the working visual reference, attach it after the
character sheets on the next request. Identify it as environment, outfit, and
art-style continuity only; the original sheets remain the identity anchors.
Do not carry a distorted face or mistaken outfit forward through page references.

These prompts propose muted full color, a 2:3 portrait canvas (target 1024 x
1536 if supported), left-to-right/top-to-bottom reading, and specific panel
arrangements for a draft proof. These are not approved publication standards
or a completed storyboard. Keep the same available canvas size on all four.

This requested workflow generates a lettered full-page draft. Exact text is
included; lettering must be checked against the script. If text is inaccurate,
correct the lettering separately rather than accepting rewritten dialogue.
For final production, retain editable lettering and review individual panels.

Ruby's plain left-ring-finger band in these prompts is an explicitly provisional
rendering convention to prevent it moving between pages. Its design and placement
remain unapproved canon. Ethan's ring practice remains open; avoid emphasizing
his ring finger and do not add a ring as a new identifying accessory.

## Page 001 Prompt

```text
Create ONE finished-looking DRAFT manga page for Reflections, Story 001,
"Three Hearts, Different Needs," page 1. Exactly FOUR panels.

REFERENCE ORDER: image 1 Ethan; image 2 Morgan; image 3 Rowan.
Use these images for identity, not their printed labels, multiple poses, or
reference-sheet layout. Preserve faces, hair, eyes, freckles, age, and build.
Only Ethan, Morgan, and Rowan are visible on this page. Ruby speaks off-panel
in the last panel; do not draw Ruby or add an extra person for her voice.

STYLE/FORMAT: 2:3 portrait, target 1024 x 1536 if available. Contemporary
Japanese seinen-inspired manga, clean expressive lines, semi-realistic anatomy,
subtle cel shading, muted natural full color, warm soft morning light. White
gutters, consistent outer margins, clear English lettering. Read LEFT TO RIGHT,
then TOP TO BOTTOM. No panel numbers, character labels, watermark, or page title.

IDENTITY/OUTFITS, unchanged between panels:
Ethan: 38, 178 cm, realistic dad bod with soft rounded middle, brown textured
high-fade haircut and full brown beard. Dark-sage short-sleeve three-button
henley, black jeans, dark casual boots. Keep ordinary adult proportions.
Morgan: 34, approximately 170 cm, athletic, layered chestnut hair below the
shoulders. Charcoal T-shirt, dark jeans, dark practical boots. NO wedding ring.
Rowan: 9, 130 cm, slender, short textured chestnut hair, no visible freckles.
Blue hoodie with orange accents, jeans, blue/orange sneakers and blue socks.
Do not copy different clothing from the identity sheets. No new permanent marks
or accessories. Do not invent an identifying wedding ring for Ethan.

SETTING: One shared-home kitchen connected to a coat/shoe nook. Establish a
simple reusable draft geography: cream walls, light wood floor, kitchen counter
and breakfast table on the left, open nook on the right, low wooden bench with
a dark-gray cushion against the nook wall, and a small round stool near its far
end. A kitchen window provides soft morning light. Keep the geography consistent.

PANEL 1 — Large full-width top panel. Morgan stands at the counter, Ethan
beside her turning toward Rowan in the nook. Rowan wears ONE sneaker; his other
foot has a blue sock. The missing sneaker peeks out beneath the bench. Morgan
looks brisk and affectionate; Ethan willingly answers with a half-smile.
Balloon order: Morgan: "Ethan. Shoes. Rowan's looking for his again."
Then Ethan: "Got it, Hun."

PANEL 2 — Middle row, left. Low medium shot: Ethan crouches and holds the
recovered sneaker out to Rowan. Show Rowan's already-shod foot. Rowan raises
an eyebrow at the shoe; Ethan responds with dry humor.
Balloon order: Rowan: "It wasn't there before."
Then Ethan: "Very sneaky shoe."

PANEL 3 — Middle row, right. Ethan is standing again at the kitchen/nook
threshold. Morgan lightly straightens his henley's folded neckline, not a
conventional shirt collar. Both look comfortable and affectionate. In the
background, Rowan sits on the bench putting on the recovered sneaker.
Morgan: "What would I do without you?"

PANEL 4 — Full-width bottom close-up. Ethan smiles genuinely, then directs
his eyes toward a voice from deeper in the nook. Only the edge of Morgan's
shoulder is visible beside him. Ruby remains entirely outside the frame.
Off-panel balloon from Ruby, tail pointing beyond the panel edge: "Ethan?"

Render only the exact dialogue above, without speaker names inside balloons.
Keep balloon order unambiguous and faces unobscured. No internal thoughts,
additional dialogue, or sound effects. End on curiosity about who called him.
```

PANEL 5 — Bottom row, right. Emphasize Ruby's small relieved smile; a little

Keep Riley's hoodie zipped after panel 2. Ethan's neckline becomes a little

and both shoulders in the same ordinary mirror, hands still at the basin.
Leave clear space for the final thought followed by the editorial chapter title.
First, internal caption: "Sometimes I wonder if I do."
Then, separate title lettering: "THREE HEARTS, DIFFERENT NEEDS"

Use quiet rectangular thought captions, not dialogue balloons. No other

## Check Each Result Before Continuing

- Correct panel count and reading order; no scene or dialogue omitted.
- Faces, haircuts, freckles, adult/child proportions, and height differences
  match the identity sheets and written scale.
- Same scene outfits; Morgan has no ring, Ruby's provisional band stays put.
- Shoe recovered on page 1, Riley's hoodie zipped on page 2, henley slightly
  crooked by page 3 and unchanged at the mirror; hands and backgrounds agree.
- Exact dialogue, speaker tails, captions, title, and readable text. Compare
  against script.md, not a previous page's potentially mistaken lettering.
- Save the selected output and note any corrections before using it as the
  next page's continuity reference. Generated drafts do not approve canon.
