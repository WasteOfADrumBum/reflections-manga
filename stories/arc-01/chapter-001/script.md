## Script Conventions

- Panels are numbered in narrative reading order. Physical reading direction,
  page dimensions, spreads, and panel rectangles remain unselected.
- Each panel inherits its page's time/location and the opening outfit state.
  Only visible characters are listed; off-panel speakers are identified separately.
- D = spoken dialogue; T = Ethan's internal thought, set as a caption; S =
  sound effect; TITLE = editorial title. IDs stay with their wording through revisions.
- Letter only the exact quoted text. Within a panel, follow the listed order;
  reserve separate balloons/captions for separate IDs. No additional narration,
  thoughts, dialogue, or sound effects are intended where none is specified.
- End-of-page hooks describe narrative momentum. Whether a hook falls across
  a physical page turn or within a spread is a later storyboard decision.
