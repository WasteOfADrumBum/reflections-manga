## Script Conventions

- Panels are numbered in narrative reading order. Physical reading direction,
  page dimensions, spreads, and panel rectangles remain unselected.
- Each panel inherits its page's time/location and the opening outfit state.
  Only visible characters are listed; off-panel speakers are identified separately.
- D = spoken dialogue; T = Ethan's internal thought, set as a caption; S =
  sound effect; TITLE = editorial title. IDs stay with their wording through revisions.
- Letter only the exact quoted text. Within a panel, follow the listed order;
  reserve separate balloons/captions for separate IDs. No additional narration,
  thoughts, dialogue, or sound effects are intended where none is specified.
- End-of-page hooks describe narrative momentum. Whether a hook falls across
  a physical page turn or within a spread is a later storyboard decision.

## Scene 01: Before Everyone Leaves

Time: One continuous morning, over several minutes; date and destination open.
Location: Shared home, kitchen and adjoining coat/shoe nook.
Cast: Ethan, Morgan, Ruby, Rowan, Riley, Avery.
Tone: Busy, affectionate, lightly funny. The family functions together.

### Page 001 — With Morgan

Page purpose: Establish Ethan's easy cooperation with Morgan, humor, and affection.
Four panels. A large establishing panel replaces the map's possible full-page
splash so the shoe exchange and closing look have separate readable beats.

#### Panel 001.1

- Visible cast: Morgan, Ethan, Rowan.
- Visual: Large, eye-level kitchen view. Morgan stands at the counter near
  breakfast bowls; Ethan is beside her, turning toward Rowan in the adjoining
  coat/shoe nook. Rowan has one sneaker on and one socked foot. A corner of
  the missing sneaker is visible beneath the low bench, a small visual joke
  rather than a puzzle the reader must solve. Morning light comes from the
  kitchen window. Ruby, Riley, and Avery are outside this framing.
- Expression/beat: Morgan is brisk and comfortable; Ethan's answering half-smile
  makes his willingness clear. Rowan is annoyed with the missing shoe, not distressed.
- Dialogue, balloon order:
  1. `P001.1.D01` — Morgan: "Ethan. Shoes. Rowan's looking for his again."
  2. `P001.1.D02` — Ethan: "Got it, Hun."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 001.2

- Visible cast: Ethan, Rowan.
- Visual: Low medium shot in the nook. Ethan crouches with the recovered
  sneaker held out to Rowan. Keep the bench and Rowan's already-shod foot
  visible so the recovery is immediately legible.
- Expression/beat: Rowan gives the shoe a suspicious raised eyebrow; Ethan
  meets his reasoning with dry humor rather than a lecture.
- Dialogue, balloon order:
  1. `P001.2.D01` — Rowan: "It wasn't there before."
  2. `P001.2.D02` — Ethan: "Very sneaky shoe."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 001.3

- Visible cast: Morgan, Ethan; Rowan small in the background.
- Visual: Medium two-shot at the kitchen/nook threshold. Ethan is upright
  again. Morgan straightens the folded edge of his henley's neckline with
  a light, familiar touch. Rowan sits on the bench putting on the recovered shoe.
- Expression/beat: Morgan's attention is on Ethan now, affectionate and amused;
  he leans comfortably into the little adjustment.
- Dialogue:
  1. `P001.3.D01` — Morgan: "What would I do without you?"
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 001.4

- Visible cast: Ethan; edge of Morgan's shoulder.
- Visual: Close-up of Ethan's answering smile. His eyes turn toward a voice
  from the other end of the nook; Ruby is not yet visible.
- Expression/beat: The smile is real. He is simply shifting his attention.
- Dialogue:
  1. `P001.4.D01` — Ruby, off-panel from the nook: "Ethan?"
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

End-of-page hook: Another person calls for the same man. Page 2 reveals the
different presence he brings to her; no danger or urgent demand is implied.

### Page 002 — With Ruby

Time/location: Seconds later, the same coat/shoe nook, looking toward its far end.
Page purpose: Show Ruby respecting Riley's independence before she asks for
reassurance. Ethan's grounded response contrasts with his playful deference.
Five panels.

#### Panel 002.1

- Visible cast: Ruby, Riley, Ethan.
- Visual: Medium view. Ruby sits on a low stool near Riley, who stands working
  at her teal hoodie's zipper. Ruby's hands rest back in her own lap; she has
  stopped reaching to help. Ethan has come alongside the bench and can see both.
- Expression/beat: Riley is determined, not helpless. Ruby gives her time,
  staying available without taking over.
- Dialogue, balloon order:
  1. `P002.1.D01` — Riley: "I can do it myself."
  2. `P002.1.D02` — Ruby: "Okay. I'm here if you need me."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 002.2

- Visible cast: Riley, with Ruby's resting hands at the edge of the frame.
- Visual: Close shot of Riley pulling the now-engaged zipper upward herself.
  The edge of her satisfied grin is visible above it. Ruby does not assist.
- Expression/beat: A small success that belongs to Riley.
- Lettering, reading order:
  1. `P002.2.S01` — Zipper SFX: "zzip"
  2. `P002.2.D01` — Riley: "See?"
- Thoughts: None.

#### Panel 002.3

- Visible cast: Ruby, Ethan; Riley and Rowan in the background.
- Visual: Medium two-shot. Ethan crouches beside the bench to meet the seated
  Ruby at eye level. Ruby looks toward him; her hands remain relaxed in her lap.
  Riley walks toward her brother without waiting for an adult assessment.
- Expression/beat: Ruby is checking her impulse to intervene, not asking Ethan
  to authorize her parenting. His posture becomes still and attentive.
- Dialogue, balloon order:
  1. `P002.3.D01` — Ruby: "Do you think I handled that right?"
  2. `P002.3.D02` — Ruby: "I nearly took over."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 002.4

- Visible cast: Ethan, Ruby.
- Visual: Closer two-shot at the same height. Ethan looks directly at Ruby;
  his hands rest loosely on his knees, with no corrective gesture toward her.
- Expression/beat: Calm confidence and affection. He recognizes something
  Ruby already did well rather than supplying the solution she supposedly lacks.
- Dialogue, balloon order:
  1. `P002.4.D01` — Ethan: "You did fine."
  2. `P002.4.D02` — Ethan: "She needed you to listen. You listened."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 002.5

- Visible cast: Ruby, part of Ethan; Morgan at the distant threshold.
- Visual: Close emphasis on Ruby's small, relieved smile. In the background,
  Morgan has appeared at the kitchen entrance to the nook, unhurried.
- Expression/beat: Ruby accepts reassurance. Morgan's relaxed posture keeps
  her arrival ordinary; no ominous shadow, jealous glare, or interruption effect.
- Dialogue:
  1. `P002.5.D01` — Ruby: "Thanks."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

End-of-page hook: Morgan enters the space shared by Ruby and Ethan. Page 3
answers how these relationships coexist through casual warmth.

### Page 003 — All of Them

Time/location: Continuous, nook and kitchen threshold, with the table beyond.
Page purpose: Make the shared family readable through behavior, with no
polyamory exposition box. Five panels.

#### Panel 003.1

- Visible cast: Morgan, Ruby, Ethan; Rowan and Riley in the background.
- Visual: Medium-wide view connecting the same doorway and bench. Morgan
  rests a hand against the doorframe. Ethan is beginning to rise; Ruby remains
  beside the stool. Rowan now wears both sneakers; Riley stands near him.
- Expression/beat: Morgan's raised eyebrow is teasing; Ruby answers with an
  easy smile. They are comfortable talking directly to each other.
- Dialogue, balloon order:
  1. `P003.1.D01` — Morgan: "Ruby. You two planning on staying in here all morning?"
  2. `P003.1.D02` — Ruby: "He's giving me a pep talk."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 003.2

- Visible cast: Morgan, Ethan, Ruby.
- Visual: Medium view. Ethan is upright. Morgan moves alongside him, looking
  past him to Ruby with affectionate amusement; Ruby has stood beside the stool.
- Expression/beat: A shared joke between friends, not a contest over Ethan.
- Dialogue:
  1. `P003.2.D01` — Morgan: "Of course he is."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 003.3

- Visible cast: Morgan, Ethan; Ruby at the edge of the composition.
- Visual: Medium close-up of Morgan giving Ethan a brief kiss on the cheek
  as she passes toward the kitchen. He tilts his face to meet it, smiling.
  Ruby's expression remains relaxed and familiar.
- Expression/beat: Ordinary spousal affection, received willingly.
- Lettering: SILENT — no speech, thoughts, narration, or SFX.

#### Panel 003.4

- Visible cast: Ruby and Ethan's hands, with enough of their sleeves to
  identify whose hands they are; Morgan's departing shoulder at the edge.
- Visual: Close-up of Ruby taking Ethan's hand. His fingers curl back around
  hers. Do not crop solely to a ring or treat this as a symbolic ring reveal.
- Expression/beat: Reciprocal affection with Ruby immediately after the kiss
  from Morgan; neither action is hidden from the other woman.
- Dialogue:
  1. `P003.4.D01` — Ruby, from above the frame: "Your shirt's crooked again."
- Thoughts/SFX: None.

#### Panel 003.5

- Visible cast: All six family members.
- Visual: Wide kitchen/nook tableau, giving this panel generous space. Ethan
  and Ruby stand together, hands still loosely linked; Morgan, beside the
  table, looks back at Ruby with a grin. Rowan and Riley stand shoulder to
  shoulder near the bench, sharing a small smile at the adults' joke. Avery
  sits at the table, happily tapping a spoon against her own palm while waiting
  with the others; she is part of the same composition, not isolated behind
  the sibling pair. Her interest is her own, not a reaction to adult tension.
- Expression/beat: Comfortable belonging. Ethan smiles at the shared joke;
  no tired expression yet. The reader can see the two distinct romantic bonds
  and the women's easy friendship in one ordinary household.
- Dialogue:
  1. `P003.5.D01` — Morgan, to Ruby: "I just fixed that."
  2. `P003.5.D02` — Ethan, with a small grin: "Thanks anyway, Morgan."
- Thoughts/SFX: None; Avery's small rhythmic movement is unlettered.

End-of-page hook: Hold the full family's warmth. The next page cuts to Ethan
without anyone beside him, asking what remains when the room becomes quiet.

## Scene 02: A Moment Without an Audience

Time: Moments later in the same morning; no departure or time jump implied.
Location: A small bathroom elsewhere in the home, exact position unselected.
Cast: Ethan only. The other five remain in the shared space off-page.
Transition proposal: Ethan has briefly stepped away to wash his hands after
retrieving the shoe. The walking, hand release, and washing occur between
scenes. No locked door, avoidance of a request, or household abandonment implied.

### Page 004 — Alone

Page purpose: End the opening on Ethan's subjective identity question, not an
objective claim that either spouse fails to love him. Four panels; allow
stillness and more space around the final image.

#### Panel 004.1

- Visible cast: Ethan and his ordinary mirror reflection.
- Visual: Medium view from behind Ethan's shoulder toward the bathroom mirror.
  He stands at the sink, tap already off, hands resting lightly at the basin's
  edge. His henley remains slightly crooked from the previous page. Neutral
  soft bathroom light; no abrupt ominous lighting or distorted reflection.
- Expression/beat: The social smile has faded naturally. He looks at himself.
- Internal caption:
  1. `P004.1.T01` — Ethan: "Morgan trusts me to follow her."
- Speech/SFX: None.

#### Panel 004.2

- Visible cast: Ethan's reflection, with his actual shoulder at the frame edge.
- Visual: Same axis, a little closer. His shoulders settle on an exhale; leave
  the mouth and brow relaxed rather than dramatizing misery.
- Expression/beat: Quiet tiredness beneath competence; both relationships
  remain loved parts of his life.
- Internal caption:
  1. `P004.2.T01` — Ethan: "Ruby trusts me to lead her."
- Speech/SFX: None.

#### Panel 004.3

- Visible cast: Ethan's reflected face.
- Visual: Close-up, eyes focused on his own reflection. Preserve recognizable
  face, beard, and ordinary morning appearance; no invented scars or visual
  symptoms. Leave breathing room around the caption.
- Expression/beat: The uncertainty is directed inward, not an accusing look
  toward an absent spouse.
- Internal caption:
  1. `P004.3.T01` — Ethan: "Sometimes I wonder which one of them actually knows me."
- Speech/SFX: None.

#### Panel 004.4

- Visible cast: Ethan and his reflection.
- Visual: Larger closing panel. Pull back enough to show his face and both
  shoulders in the same unbroken mirror. He is alone, hands still at the sink;
  reserve clear negative space for the last thought and title. No split face,
  alternate selves, cracked glass, or literal identity twist.
- Expression/beat: A searching, unresolved look. He has not reached the later
  realization that both relational roles are authentic parts of him.
- Lettering, reading order:
  1. `P004.4.T01` — Ethan, internal caption: "Sometimes I wonder if I do."
  2. `P004.4.TITLE01` — Editorial title: "THREE HEARTS, DIFFERENT NEEDS"
- Speech/SFX: None.

End-of-page hook into page 005: Who is Ethan when nobody is asking him to
be someone? The planned next sequence returns to the family's warmth and
the real pleasures of both relationships, giving that question complexity
rather than answering it with a crisis.

## Draft Choices and Review Focus

- Proposed page-2 incident: Riley zips her own hoodie while Ruby respects her
  request for independence. This fills an open outline detail; it is not an
  approved parenting event or claim that Ruby routinely doubts her competence.
- Proposed small additions: shoe joke, Ruby's "I nearly took over," and the
  crooked-shirt exchange. Original core reassurance and four mirror thoughts
  are retained. Ethan uses his established pet name Hun.
- Morgan straightens a henley neckline instead of a conventional collar,
  matching the proposed outfit. Page 1 uses four panels rather than a splash.
- Avery's spoon play is a proposed expression of her music/movement interest,
  not a communication system, sensory symptom, or established recurring habit.
- The bathroom and between-scene hand-washing transition are draft staging.
  No house floor plan, season, calendar day, school, or destination is fixed.
- These pages introduce the children visually; the more substantial individual
  child beats on pages 011–015 remain to be written. No child mediates adult strain.
- Review the emotional balance, the clarity of both romantic bonds, natural
  dialogue, and the final mirror beat before continuing pages 005–010.
- Storyboard flow, physical page turns, lettering fit, and artwork QA have not
  been performed. No final image dimensions or selected reference versions yet.
