## Story

Ethan loves two very different women, but being a different version of himself with each creates an internal struggle.

## Plot

Morgan expects Ethan to trust her leadership, while Ruby looks to Ethan for reassurance and direction. Both women love him, but Ethan begins feeling that neither fully knows the person he is when he is alone.

## Intended Outcome

Ethan realizes he can love and support both women without surrendering his identity.
