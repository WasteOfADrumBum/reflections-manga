## Story

Morgan communicates love through passion, expectations, and directness. Ruby communicates through gentleness, affection, and caregiving.

## Plot

Ethan tries to satisfy both, but Morgan feels challenged when he disagrees with her, and Ruby sometimes feels overlooked when he is preoccupied with Morgan.

## Intended Outcome

All three discover that being loved and feeling loved are not always the same thing.
