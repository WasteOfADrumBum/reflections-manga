## Story

Ethan notices he is always initiating affection with Morgan while Ruby naturally seeks closeness with him.

## Plot

He stops reaching for Morgan, hoping she will notice. Morgan interprets his withdrawal as punishment, while Ruby feels guilty for receiving the affection he wants from Morgan.

## Intended Outcome

Ethan learns that withdrawing affection as a test creates more distance, but Morgan also recognizes that he needs to feel wanted.
