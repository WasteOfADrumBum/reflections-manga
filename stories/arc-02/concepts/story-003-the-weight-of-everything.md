## Story

Ethan begins carrying more of the household responsibilities while Morgan pursues personal interests and Ruby focuses on Avery's additional needs.

## Plot

Ethan becomes exhausted but hesitates to ask for help. Morgan believes he is capable, Ruby assumes he would speak up, and neither recognizes how overwhelmed he has become.

## Intended Outcome

Ethan finally breaks down, forcing all three to confront the difference between sharing a home and sharing its burdens.

## Continuity and Development Notes

The established opening baseline has Ethan working full-time, Morgan part-time, and Ruby handling most daily childcare. This story depicts a period of shifted or increased load; establish that change instead of rewriting the baseline. Show Morgan's and Ruby's pressures too. Avery remains a person with agency and interests, not a burden or the cause of adult conflict.

Morgan and Ruby's independent friendship is platonic. Show the children as
individual people with continuing interests and relationships; no child is a
prop, mediator, or explanation for the adults' entire conflict.
