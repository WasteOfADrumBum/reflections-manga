## Story

Ethan struggles to finish expressing his thoughts during emotionally intense disagreements with Morgan.

## Plot

Morgan interprets his repeated explanations as arguments. Ruby tries to mediate but unintentionally interrupts him too. Ethan eventually begins writing down everything he cannot say.

## Intended Outcome

Ruby discovers his writings and helps Morgan understand the thoughts beneath his frustration, though understanding does not immediately resolve their differences.
