## Story

Morgan's unworn wedding ring becomes an emotional symbol for Ethan while Ruby continues wearing hers.

## Plot

Ethan quietly compares the two, assigning meaning to something Morgan considers practical. Ruby challenges him to recognize that her ring does not make her love more genuine.

## Intended Outcome

Ethan learns not to measure devotion through symbols alone, while Morgan acknowledges why the missing ring hurts him.
