## Story

Morgan starts rediscovering herself outside marriage and motherhood.

## Plot

Her growing independence makes Ethan feel less important. Ruby initially encourages Morgan but eventually admits that she, too, has sacrificed parts of her identity.

## Intended Outcome

Morgan and Ruby begin exploring their individuality, while Ethan learns that loving them means making room for their growth without abandoning his own.
