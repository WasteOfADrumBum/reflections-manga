## Story

Ethan and Morgan experience intense physical closeness after emotionally exhausting arguments, while Ruby wants intimacy built on peace and reassurance.

## Plot

Ethan begins noticing that the strongest passion in one marriage follows conflict, whereas the other offers steadier emotional connection.

## Intended Outcome

He realizes that passion and emotional security are not interchangeable, and all three begin questioning how they create intimacy.
