## Story

Morgan chooses more privacy and independence, which Ethan experiences as another sign of emotional withdrawal.

## Plot

Ruby understands Ethan's desire for reassurance but also understands Morgan's need for autonomy. When a family emergency occurs, they confront the practical and emotional meaning of staying connected.

## Intended Outcome

They establish boundaries around privacy and safety without treating access to each other's whereabouts as proof of love.
