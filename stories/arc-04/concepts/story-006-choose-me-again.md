## Story

After years of marriage and parenting, all three have begun confusing commitment with obligation.

## Plot

Ethan wants affection without asking. Morgan wants freedom without guilt. Ruby wants a peaceful family without constantly mediating.

## Intended Outcome

They decide that being married is not enough. Each must consciously choose the relationship and the work it requires, without pretending that love guarantees everything will work out.
