## Workflow

All concepts share one continuity. Carry approved consequences into later
stories instead of resetting relationships. Planned plots and intended outcomes
remain drafts until chapter development and explicit approval.

Each chapter contains an outline, script,
storyboard, continuity notes, and approved pages.

Only approved developments enter the series canon.
